Compiled 2026-10-07. This file reports only what sources show or say. It contains no design opinions or recommendations.

- **Instagram Instants: NOT researched.** The shared WebSearch budget for this session (200 calls across all agents) ran out after this agent's 34th query. Every Instagram Instants item is **UNKNOWN**. See section 2 and the Gaps section.

- **Do not fill these gaps from memory.** A follow-up research pass needs to run the query list in the Gaps section.

## Method and how to read citations

- **Tool.** The only tool used was WebSearch. Page bodies were never opened, because the network blocks App Store pages, company sites, press sites, Reddit, Medium and Wikipedia.
- **What the evidence is.** Every fact below comes from a search engine's result summary or snippet. Each fact is cited to the URL or URLs that the engine returned with that statement.
- **Attribution limits.** Some results returned several URLs together. In those cases, matching a fact to one URL is best-effort, and the citation lists the most likely URL(s).
- **Confidence tags:**
  - `[multi]`: two or more independent sources agree.
  - `[single]`: one source.
  - `[inferred]`: the researcher's interpretation. These are kept rare and are labelled.
  - `[conflict]`: sources disagree. Every version is reported.
- **UNKNOWN** means no source in this pass stated the value. Hex codes, font names and copy that no source gave are marked UNKNOWN and were never filled in by guessing.

### 1.8 BTS (Behind The Scenes)

All facts in this section come from one official help-center article: https://help.bereal.com/hc/articles/15272815079453. Tag: [single] (official) unless noted otherwise.

- BTS shares "those few seconds leading up to your BeReal". The help article compares it to an iOS Live Photo.
- **Only the first camera chosen** records the BTS video.
- Recording starts when the camera screen opens and keeps the last few seconds before capture.
- BTS is **enabled by default**.
- The toggle sits in the **top right** and is labelled **"BTS On"** / **"BTS Off"**.
- **Long-press** the BeReal to preview the BTS.
- A **mute button** disables the BTS sound.
- The post button is labelled **"SEND"** (all caps, as quoted).
- Viewers see a **Live Photo symbol** in the upper corner of the image and **long-press** to play it.
