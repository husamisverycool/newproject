# 22 — Recaps, memories and print books (beyond Spotify Wrapped): exact strings, layout, flows

Research date: 2026-10-07. Method: WebSearch only (**24 searches, the hard limit**). No curl, WebFetch or Firecrawl. Results arrive as the search engine's summaries of pages, not raw page text. A quoted string is verbatim only as far as the summary quoted it. When a result set held several URLs and the summary did not say which one a sentence came from, every candidate URL is listed. Check every row against a live screen before shipping.

**Scope.** This file does not repeat dossier 15 §1 or dossier 04 §1 (Spotify Wrapped 2025), the Google Photos Memories cut-out-number redesign (06 §1.7), the Google Photos Create tab (06 §1, 16 §1) or Retro Rewind and recaps (02 §B4.6–B4.7). It adds new sources only.

**Tag legend** (same as dossier 15)
- **[V]**: from a first-party page (the company's own support site, blog or store listing) whose wording the summary quoted or reproduced (for example a first-person "we" sentence).
- **[V-weak]**: from third-party press, tutorials or reviews, or a first-party page that the summary paraphrased. Treat the wording as approximate.
- **[B]**: background knowledge I could not verify. It appears only in the separate section near the end.
- **NOT FOUND**: searched for but not in any result.

**Our surfaces (spec):** §G weekly AI recap (collage, slideshow with transitions, music and export, panels) · §L on-this-day ("N years ago") and the time-hop widget · §V annual group Wrapped (story player, roles, awards, "guess whose photo" quiz, "five days that defined our year", party mode) · Yearbook (printed book).
