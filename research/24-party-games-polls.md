# Dossier 24: Party games, polls, AI group games: exact strings

Compiled: 2026-10-07. Tool: WebSearch only, "standard" mode. **24 of 24 allowed searches were used.** No page was opened directly. curl and WebFetch are blocked by policy, and this dossier did not work around that.

Scope: social and party game apps, and AI-driven group games, that were **not** covered in `16-ai-games-messaging-exact.md` §5–8 (tbh/Gas, Gartic Phone, Wordle, Jackbox) or `15-wrapped-partiful-exact.md` §1e (Spotify Wrapped Party). Rows that only repeat those dossiers are left out. Where this round adds to a source they cover, the row says "adds to 16 §x".

## Method and how to read the tags

- Every verified row comes from a WebSearch result summary and its result URLs, not from the full page. A summary does not always say which URL a sentence came from. In those cases the row cites the most likely URL, or the result set it came from.
- Text inside quotation marks is quoted as the search result returned it. Capitalization and punctuation **may differ from the live UI**. "Verified" means verified as documented, not checked against a screenshot.
- Tags (same scheme as dossier 16):
  - **[V]**: an official or primary source (support.*.com, the company's blog or newsroom, the App Store listing, the official game site), or two or more independent sources that agree on the exact string.
  - **[V-weak]**: one secondary source (a tutorial, a fan wiki, a news paraphrase), or sources that agree on the meaning but not the exact wording.
  - **[B]**: background knowledge that was not verified. These rows appear **only** in the separate "BACKGROUND KNOWLEDGE [B]" section, each with a confidence rating.
- **Echo warning.** Some searches put candidate strings in the query, for example the Kahoot hex codes or the Connections emoji. When a summary only repeated the query back without a source confirming it, the value is **not** counted as verified. These cases are marked.
- Surfaces use the spec §K letters: **(a)** positive superlative polls, **(b)** photo telephone with replay, **(c)** weekly photo challenges, **(d)** "guess whose photo" quiz, **(e)** regenerating group awards, **(f)** spoiler-free share grid, **(g)** host and audience mode for more than 10 players, **(h)** group quests.

## Risk notes for the product team (factual, from the rows above)

- **Trademarks and proprietary assets.** "Kahoot!", the four answer shapes paired with their colors as Kahoot trade dress, "Quiplash" / "Thriplash" / "The Last Lash", "Heads Up!", "Psych!", "Among Us" / "Impostor" art, "Connections", "Add Yours", and Discord's rocket launcher all belong to their owners. Copying names, art or music verbatim needs legal review. Copying the patterns and mechanics is a separate question from copying the strings.
- **Positive-only rule.** The verified Gas sample includes a negative question ("Who's most likely to punch someone"). Psych!'s "Truth Comes Out" prompts are teasing, not complimentary. Filter both before use.
- **Anonymity dark patterns.** The Sendit behavior TechCrunch reported (questions arriving with no real sender) is a pattern to **avoid**. Our polls show names, which is the opposite design.
