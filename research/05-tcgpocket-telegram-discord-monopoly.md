**Rare Pack ("God Pack")**
- Probability is 0.05% (1 in 2,000).
- The official name is "Rare Pack". It contains only ☆ Art Rare and above.
- Per-card distribution inside a Rare Pack: Ultra Rare 5%, Immersive Rare 5%, Special Art Rare and Super Rare 50%, Art Rare 40%.
- A Rare Pack can be shown to friends as a Wonder Pick.
- Sources: https://ptcgpocket.gg/rare-packs/ ; https://www.pockettactics.com/pokemon-tcg-pocket/god-packs ; https://esports.gg/news/pokemon-tcg-pocket/what-is-a-god-pack-in-pokemon-tcg-pocket/ ; https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/what-is-a-god-pack-in-pokemon-tcg-pocket-2984966 [single]

## 1.5 Pack-opening sequence (in order)

1. **Choose a pack.** A new account picks one of three Genetic Apex packs: Mewtwo, Pikachu or Charizard. Source: https://www.oneesports.gg/gaming/best-packs-to-open-in-pokemon-tcg-pocket ; https://www.nintendolife.com/guides/pokemon-tcg-pocket-which-booster-packs-should-you-open [multi]
2. **Pick the physical-looking pack.** The button label is "Open Booster Pack". Per GameWith, "the gacha sequence ends when you press the 'Open Booster Pack' button, making the selection of Booster Packs purely for show". Source: https://gamewith.net/pokemon-tcg-pocket/49002 ; https://gamewith.net/pokemon-tcg-pocket/49055 [single]
   - The layout of the pack-selection screen (how many packs are shown, carousel or grid) was not retrieved: UNKNOWN.
3. **Swipe to cut.** "Users swipe a finger across the top of the digital package, and the sensation of opening the physical package is realistically re-created in sound and vibration." Source: https://corporate.pokemon.co.jp/en/topics/detail/t-28/ ; https://malaya.com.ph/lifestyle/living/pokemon-trading-card-game-pocket-officially-debuts ; https://nintendowire.com/?p=201514 [multi]
   - Guide wording: "slide your finger across the screen towards the top of the card pack ... complete with the satisfying sound of the packet ripping." Source: https://gamewith.net/pokemon-tcg-pocket/49002 [single]
4. **Rare-card tells during opening.** GameWith reports:
   - "a guaranteed animation where a cracking sound plays when opening a Booster Pack" that holds a rare card. The sound is easy to miss when the device is muted.
   - "a guaranteed animation of a glowing light when opening a Booster Pack with a rare card".
   - "a shining effect when you swipe a Booster Pack".
   - Source: https://gamewith.net/pokemon-tcg-pocket/49002 [single]
   - Which rarity threshold sets these off, and what colour the glow is: UNKNOWN.
   - GameWith also tested the rumour that a bent or crimped pack edge means a rare card. It "found no guarantee". Source: same [single]
5. **Card reveal.** One digest mentions a "'Tap and hold' reveal animation". Source: https://www.lemon8-app.com/@loretraders/7472437997116473899?region=us [single, low quality: user post]
   - One-at-a-time reveal, fan-out of five, slow-down for rare cards, "NEW" badge: **no source found. UNKNOWN.**
6. **Finish.** "After opening the pack and seeing what five cards you've pulled, you'll be prompted to 'swipe up' to add them to your card dex." Source: https://gamewith.net/pokemon-tcg-pocket/49002 (result set) [single]
7. **Pack Points.** Every booster pack opened adds 5 Pack Points (see 1.10). Source: https://www.pokemon-zone.com/articles/pack-points/ [single]
