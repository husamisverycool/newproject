## How to read the tags

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[V]** | The wording appears in search excerpts in a way that ties it to a named source: a store listing, a company post, or a named outlet quoting it. It was seen in **2 or more separate searches or sources** with the same wording. |
| **[V-weak]** | Seen once, or the search summariser may have paraphrased it, or the exact page it came from could not be isolated. Treat it as "reported wording" and check it on a device before shipping. |
| **[B]** | Background knowledge that I could **not** verify. Listed only in the separate [B] section at the end. |

**Important caveat.** Fetching pages directly was blocked, so every string here comes through a search engine's summary of the page. Store "What's New" text is the most reliable kind of string, because it is the app maker's own wording. Even so, punctuation such as em dashes versus hyphens, and capitalisation, may differ from the live UI. **None of these strings were checked against real screens.**
